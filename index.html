About Us

Built by teachers, not a franchise

Infignia is a Cambridge-curriculum tuition academy based in Lahore. We are not just running batches; we are shaping how O Level and A Level students actually learn Mathematics, the Sciences, Economics and Computer Science. Founded on the belief that large classrooms and generic notes were failing capable students, we started Infignia in 2023 with a simple mission: teach the exam honestly.

Every course we run stays capped at twelve students, taught by someone who specialises in that exact subject at that exact level. Our commitment doesn't stop at covering the syllabus — it's about turning genuine understanding into a grade that reflects it.
