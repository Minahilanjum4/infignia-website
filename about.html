Our Story

Company Overview

Infignia is a Cambridge-curriculum tuition academy based in Lahore, teaching O Level and A Level students across Mathematics, the Sciences, Economics and Computer Science. I started Infignia with a small team of subject teachers who were tired of watching capable students underperform simply because their batches were too large, or their practice too generic.

Every course we run is capped at twelve students, taught by someone who specialises in that exact subject at that exact level, and built around one habit: solving real, timed past papers until the exam itself stops feeling unfamiliar. We keep the academy small on purpose — it's the only way we can keep our word on every student's progress report.

What We Stand For

Mission & Vision

Mission

Teach the exam honestly

To give every O Level and A Level student a genuine grasp of their subject, and the exam technique to turn that grasp into a grade that actually reflects it — no shortcuts, no spoon-feeding.

Vision

A small academy that stays small

To remain the kind of academy where every teacher knows every student by name — growing by adding subject specialists, never by adding students past the point where anyone can actually learn.

We Make Exam Prep Simple

Fewer distractions, better feedback — that's how a grade actually improves.
